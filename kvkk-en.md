# KVKK and GDPR Information Notice

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") to inform you about personal data processing related to the Payliva app.

## 1. Data controller

Taha Efe Kusoglu
E-mail: [enterbossapp@gmail.com](mailto:enterbossapp@gmail.com)

## 2. Data you enter in the App

The income, expenses, rates, categories, notes, display name and settings you enter in Payliva are stored only on your device and, when iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud account as an automatic backup. We have no access to this data; we do not collect, transfer or process it. We therefore carry out no processing of personal data with respect to this data.

Apple operates in-app purchases and the iCloud service independently under its own privacy policy.

## 3. Personal data processed

The following data is processed only when you contact us by e-mail:

- Identity data: your name (if you include it)
- Contact data: your e-mail address
- Request data: the content of your message and any attachments

## 4. Purposes

This data is processed only to:

- receive and answer your support requests, suggestions and complaints,
- handle requests regarding your personal data,
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims in case of a dispute.

## 5. Method of collection and legal basis

Your personal data is collected electronically when you send us an e-mail and is processed on the following legal bases:

- Our legitimate interest in answering your request, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))
- Compliance with our legal obligations, such as answering data protection requests (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç); GDPR Art. 6(1)(c))
- The establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (KVKK Art. 5(2)(e); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

## 6. Transfers

Your personal data is not transferred to third parties for marketing or any other commercial purpose.

Correspondence is stored on the systems of the provider of our e-mail service. This provider's servers may be located abroad; in that case the transfer is made in accordance with Article 9 of KVKK and Chapter V of the GDPR.

Data may be shared with legally authorised public authorities only when we are legally required to and only to the extent requested.

## 7. Retention

Support correspondence is kept for a reasonable period required by its purpose after your request is resolved and is then deleted. If there is a dispute or legal obligation, it may be kept for the period set out in the applicable law.

## 8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK and Articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR, you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it,
- learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
- know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad,
- access your data and receive a copy of it,
- have incomplete or inaccurate data rectified,
- have your data erased or destroyed,
- request that rectification or erasure be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
- restrict processing and object to processing based on legitimate interest,
- object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis of your data,
- data portability, where applicable,
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## 9. How to exercise your rights

You can send your request in writing, or by e-mail to [enterbossapp@gmail.com](mailto:enterbossapp@gmail.com) from an e-mail address you have previously shared with us. Please state your name, contact details and your request clearly.

We will respond free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If the request requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged under the tariff set by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) under Article 14 of KVKK, or, if you are in the European Union, with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence.
