# Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

## The App uses no cookies

The Payliva mobile app does not use cookies, pixels, device fingerprinting or similar tracking technologies. No web pages are shown inside the App, and it contains no ads or analytics tools.

The App stores your settings and data only in its own database on your device, solely so that it can work. This information is not used to identify or track you and does not leave your device.

## Our web page

The web page where these texts and our support information are published does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

The provider hosting the web page may keep technically necessary access logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of access) under its own policies to deliver the page securely and reliably. We do not use these logs to identify you.

## Links

Links in the App or on our web page (for example to Apple's pages) may take you to other sites. Those sites' own cookie policies apply.
